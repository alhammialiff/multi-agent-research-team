### Poem: "Ode to Our Canine Companions"

In fields of gold they run and play,  
Our loyal friends, both night and day.  
With eyes aglow and hearts so true,  
These are the dogs, companions through.

Compassion blooms in every glance,  
An unspoken bond, a sacred dance.  
Their love like rivers, deep and wide,  
In their embrace, we do confide.

A steadfast guard at every door,  
Their loyalty leaves us wanting more.  
With every wag and gentle nudge,  
They heal our hearts, they bear no grudge.

The playful leaps and joyful barks,  
Chase away the lonely darks.  
A bounding spirit, a burst of light,  
In their presence, all feels right.

With courage bold, they stand so near,  
A guardian presence we hold dear.  
Their watchful gaze, a shielded stand,  
Brings peace and comfort, strong yet grand.

Within our homes, they claim a part,  
Etched within the human heart.  
With gentle paws, they make their mark,  
A friendship's flame, forever spark.

So here's to them, our faithful friends,  
Whose love and care know no end.  
In every tail wag, a story spun,  
The bond with dogs, cannot be undone.
