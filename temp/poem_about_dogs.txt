Joyful companions, loyal and true,\nWith wagging tails and eyes so blue.\nRunning in fields, so full of play,\nChasing the worries of life away.\n\nWith every bark, a song they sing,\nOf friendship, love, and the joy they bring.\nBy our side through night and day,\nIn their hearts, they forever stay.\n\nA wag of the tail, a soft gentle paw,\nA nuzzle for comfort, a gaze of awe.\nIn their presence, our hearts do thrive,\nWith them, our spirits come alive.\n\nOh, dogs, our faithful, furry friends,\nIn your company, joy never ends.\nTogether through life’s winding bends,\nThis bond, so pure, it transcends.
